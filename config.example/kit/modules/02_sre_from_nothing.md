title: Building an SRE practice from nothing
use_when: [sre, site reliability, reliability, uptime, on-call, incident, observability, slo]
---
When I joined Acme Learning there was no on-call rotation; outages were handled by whoever noticed first, usually a customer. I started with the basics: a paid, humane on-call rotation, a blameless incident review within two business days, and dashboards that answered "is it broken?" before anyone asked. Within a year we had SLOs for all twelve customer-facing services, error budgets that product managers actually used in planning, and a quarterly reliability review with the CTO. Major incidents fell by sixty percent, and the review notes became the most-read engineering document in the company.
