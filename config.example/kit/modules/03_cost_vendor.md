title: Cost discipline & vendor negotiation
use_when: [cost, finops, efficiency, budget, vendor, cloud spend, unit economics]
---
I own a four-million-dollar annual cloud budget at Acme Learning, and I treat it the way a product team treats a funnel. I gave every team a monthly cost report tied to the services they own, rightsized the largest clusters, and moved steady workloads onto savings plans after three months of usage data. We cut AWS spend by thirty percent in a year without slowing a single roadmap item. I also renegotiated our observability and CDN contracts at renewal by bringing usage data to the table, which saved another four hundred thousand dollars a year.
