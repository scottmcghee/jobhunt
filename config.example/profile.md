# Candidate profile — Jordan Example

This is an EXAMPLE profile for a fictional candidate. Replace all of it with your own. The scorer
reads this file verbatim, so write it for a skeptical recruiter: concrete scope, numbers, and an
honest list of gaps. Keep the "Known gaps" heading; the scoring rubric refers to it by name.

## Target

- Director or VP of Engineering, leading infrastructure, platform, SRE, or data organizations.
- Remote within the US, or hybrid in the Seattle area.
- Preferred industries: education, healthcare, public sector. Open to others with a clear mission.

## Credentials

- B.S. Computer Science.
- AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional.

## Most recent — Acme Learning, Director of Platform Engineering (2019–present)

- Leads a 28-person org: platform engineering, SRE, and data engineering (three managers, plus ICs).
- Reports to the CTO; member of the engineering leadership team.
- Built the SRE practice from zero: on-call, incident review, SLOs for the 12 customer-facing services.
- Cut CI pipeline time from 40 minutes to 9 by moving builds to remote caching and ephemeral runners.
- Owns a $4M annual cloud budget; reduced AWS spend 30% in one year through rightsizing and commitments.
- Led migration of the analytics warehouse from on-prem Postgres to Snowflake.

## Example Health — Engineering Manager, Infrastructure (2014–2019)

- Managed 8 engineers running AWS infrastructure for a HIPAA-regulated patient-messaging product.
- Introduced Terraform and moved deployments from manual runbooks to CI/CD.

## Earlier roles

- Senior software engineer and team lead at two SaaS companies (2006–2014), backend and build tooling.

## People leadership record

- Hired 20+ engineers and promoted four into management or staff roles.
- Has not yet led through a layer of Directors.

## Known gaps (be honest in scoring)

- No production Kubernetes ownership; container workloads ran on ECS.
- Single-cloud (AWS) only; no GCP or Azure.
- Not hands-on in application code for the last five years.
- No direct ML platform or LLM product experience.

## What a 9–10 looks like

A Director or VP role owning infrastructure, platform, or SRE at a mission-driven company of 200–2,000
people, remote-friendly, on AWS, where the core problem is reliability, developer productivity, or cost.

## What a 1–3 looks like

Individual-contributor roles, sales or marketing leadership, roles whose core requirement is a known gap
(for example, deep Kubernetes ownership), or onsite roles outside the target locations.
